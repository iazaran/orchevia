# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Orchevia a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes: using welcoming language, respecting viewpoints, gracefully
accepting constructive criticism, focusing on what is best for the community, and showing
empathy.

Unacceptable behavior includes: sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insulting or
derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment, and
publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers. All
complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject
contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior deemed
inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (repository, issues, discussions,
releases) and in public spaces when representing the project.
